Experimental mode

ULTIMATE-FILE-CONVERTER includes an opt-in Experimental mode (off by default) that performs raw-byte reinterpretation between any source and target format. Output from this mode may be invalid, nonsensical, or unusable as the claimed format. By enabling it, you accept all responsibility for the resulting files and any consequences of using or distributing them.
